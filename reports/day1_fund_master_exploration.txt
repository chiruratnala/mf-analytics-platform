Notes on AMFI codes:
  - Each amfi_code is a unique numeric ID assigned by AMFI to a single scheme+plan
    combination (e.g. Regular Growth and Direct Growth of the same fund get
    DIFFERENT amfi_codes, even though the underlying portfolio is identical).
  - This is confirmed by fund_house: SBI Bluechip Regular (119551) vs
    SBI Bluechip Direct (119552) — same scheme, different code, different plan.
  - amfi_code is the primary join key linking fund_master to nav_history,
    scheme_performance, transactions, and holdings.

Example — Regular vs Direct plan pairs (same scheme_name root):

    119552  SBI Bluechip Fund - Direct Plan - Growth  Direct               0.66
